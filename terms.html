creator-authorised content to the user's own authorised YouTube channel.

The user remains responsible for:

  • the video and metadata submitted to YouTube;
  • holding the necessary rights and permissions;

Prohibited use

Project Bonga must not be used to:

  • upload content without the necessary rights;
  • manipulate views, likes, comments or subscriptions;
  • evade YouTube restrictions or enforcement;
  • collect Google or YouTube passwords;
  • download or separate audio or video from YouTube content;
  • harass, surveil or profile viewers;
  • violate YouTube API Services Terms or Developer Policies.

Disconnection

The user may disconnect Project Bonga and delete its local YouTube API records.

This does not delete data held by YouTube.
